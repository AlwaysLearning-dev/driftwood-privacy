Driftwood: Relay Link — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Driftwood: Relay Link does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no third-party SDKs that communicate with any server.
  • Your story progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Notifications: with your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device so the story can continue in real time. These are generated on the device; no notification service or server is involved.
  • Children: the app is designed to be suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
  • Permissions used: notifications (optional, asked in-story) and exact alarms (optional, so timed messages arrive on time). Nothing else.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete beyond the on-device save, which you control by uninstalling the app or using "start over" on the title screen.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.

Driftwood: Relay Link is an original interactive fiction game. This page exists so app stores can link to the policy.
